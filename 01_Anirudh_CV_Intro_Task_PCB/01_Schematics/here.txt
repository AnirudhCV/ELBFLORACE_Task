i.    Using 24pc 150141BS73140 WL-SMTW SMT Mono-color TOP LED PLCC4 Common Cathode as BLUE LED's (Luminous Intensity 700mcd - 1000mcd)
ii.   Using 24pc 150141YS73140 WL-SMTW SMT Mono-color TOP LED PLCC4 Common Cathode as YELLOW LED's (Luminous Intensity 1500mcd - 2000mcd)
iii.  Forward Voltage for Blue LED is in the range 3.2V - 3.5V
iv.   Forward Voltage for Yellow LED is in the range 2.4V - 2.8V
v.    Both LED's have continuous forward current as 50mA and Peak forward current as 100mA
vi.   I have taken 3313J-1-102E resistor as it was the smallest on PCB footprint and I assume if CCW and WIPER are connected together, according to the datasheet, the CW gives complete resistance
vii.  100 ohm resistors are used in series connection with the Blue LED's and 220 ohm resistor are used in series connection with the Yellow LED's
viii. Max current at Blue LED = 48mA and Yellow LED = 43.64mA
ix.   Max total input power = 4.608W (Blue cascade) and 4.189W (Yellow cascade)
